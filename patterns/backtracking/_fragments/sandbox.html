Decision tree runner

Pick a template, type whole numbers separated by commas, and run. Watch the tree grow, the path change, and branches get cut.

Try an example:

For the examples, the counts match patterns/backtracking/measure.py: subsets of 1, 2, 3 visit 8 nodes; with the skip, 1, 2, 2 gives 6 answers and skips 2 branches; orders of 1, 2, 3 visit 16 nodes; 2, 3, 5 to 8 visits 13 nodes with 7 cuts; 2, 3, 6, 7 to 7 visits 10 nodes with 7 cuts; 3, 5 to 4 visits 2 nodes with 2 cuts. Long runs stop recording after 60 steps, but the counts cover the whole run.
