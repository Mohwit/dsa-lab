Window playground

Pick a window, type your own input, and step through it. The readout compares the window's work with the brute force.

These counts match patterns/sliding-window/measure.py. At most k different: 13 vs 24, 8 vs 19, 4 vs 10 and 9 vs 18 (window moves vs brute force reads). Fixed size: 16 vs 28, 8 vs 8 and 9 vs 12 touches. With k = 2 a slide costs as much as a recount; the window wins as k grows.
