Greedy scanner

Pick a template, type a list of whole numbers separated by commas, and run it. The trace shows each greedy choice; the readout races it against brute force.

These example counts match patterns/greedy/measure.py. Max subarray: all negative gives -1 (4 steps vs 10 run totals), all positive 14 (5 vs 15), dip then climb 7 (5 vs 15). Jumps: stuck on a zero is False at check 4, one spot is True after 1 check, big first jump is True after 7 checks. For jumps the brute force here is cheap on short lists; its cost grows with the jump lengths, up to about n2 / 2 marks.
