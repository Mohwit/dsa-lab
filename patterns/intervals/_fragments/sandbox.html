Interval runner

Pick a template and type intervals as start-end pairs, like 1-3, 2-5, 8-10 (or [1,3],[2,5]). Times go from 0 to 30, and each end must be bigger than its start.

For the examples, the counts match patterns/intervals/measure.py (section 7): the lesson example merges with 4 scan checks against 10 pair checks; five meetings need 3 rooms with 10 events against 25 tests; remove fewest drops 2 after 4 checks against 32 sets tried; the touching chain needs 1 room and merges into [1, 7].
