Matrix lab

Type a grid: numbers separated by spaces, rows separated by semicolons. Then rotate it, walk it in a spiral, or set its zeroes, and step through the trace.

These match patterns/math-geometry/measure.py: rotating 1..9 gives 7 4 1 | 8 5 2 | 9 6 3; its spiral is 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 8, 7, 4, 5; one row stays 1, 2, 3, 4; the middle zero gives 1 0 1 | 0 0 0 | 1 0 1; the corner zero gives 0 0 0 | 0 4 5.
