Bit lab

Two templates, your input. Count the 1s of any 32-bit number with n &= n - 1, or fold a list with XOR and watch pairs cancel.

These match patterns/bit-manipulation/measure.py. Drop steps: 44 takes 3, 0 takes 0, 255 takes 8, 4096 takes 1, -1 takes 32 (after masking); checking all positions is always 32. XOR lists: 4 in 5 steps (dict: 8 ops), 9 in 1 (dict: 2), 8 in 7 (dict: 11). On 6, 6, 5, 3 XOR gives 6 (that is 5 ^ 3) while the dict finds 5: the "pairs plus one" promise is broken.
