Top-k triage

Type whole numbers separated by commas, and k. Run it to watch a heap of size k keep the k largest, and compare its comparisons with sorting everything.

For the five examples, these counts match patterns/heap/measure.py (heap vs sort comparisons): 13 vs 25, 4 vs 7, 5 vs 10, 17 vs 12 and 7 vs 12. On an increasing list every number gets into the heap, so on tiny inputs the sort can win.
