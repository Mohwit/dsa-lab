Two pointer playground

Pick a template, type your own input, and step through it. The pair search also counts what trying every pair would cost.

For the four pair examples, these counts match patterns/two-pointers/measure.py: 6 vs 16, 4 vs 10, 3 vs 1 and 3 vs 6 (two pointer checks vs brute force checks). On "Repeats" the brute force wins, because its very first pair is the answer. Big-O is about how the worst case grows. The palindrome checks need 3, 4, 7 and 0 comparisons.
