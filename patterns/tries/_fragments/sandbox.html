Trie workshop

Type your own words, separated by commas. Watch the trie grow, then look something up in it. In a wildcard search, a dot matches any one letter.

Node and letter counts for the example word lists match patterns/tries/measure.py (for example cat, car, card, care, dog: 17 letters, 9 nodes; cat, cot, cut, cup, dog with ..g: 8 calls).
