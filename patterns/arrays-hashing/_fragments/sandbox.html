Seen-it scanner

Type a list of whole numbers, separated by commas. Run it to watch the set fill up and stop at the first repeat.

For the four example lists, these counts match patterns/arrays-hashing/measure.py: 6 vs 15, 3 vs 2, 7 vs 21, and 2 vs 1 (steps vs comparisons). On a lucky early repeat the brute force can win on tiny lists. Big-O is about how the worst case grows.
