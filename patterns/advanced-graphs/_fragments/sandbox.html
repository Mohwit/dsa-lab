Shortest paths and MST lab

Type an undirected weighted graph as edges like A-B 4, separated by commas. Pick a start node, then run Dijkstra (cheapest cost to every node) or Prim (cheapest tree that connects them).

For the five examples, patterns/advanced-graphs/measure.py prints the same distances and counts. Two islands: X and Y stay at inf, and Prim reports that the graph is not connected. A negative weight is refused for Dijkstra (see Trap 2) but allowed for Prim.
